# Content SEO

Combines three skills adapted from github.com/Illyism/seo-skills (Ilias Ism, MIT license): blog-refresher, seo-title-optimization and customer-journey.

## General rules

- Always do real research (Google, Reddit, Quora, industry forums), never rely on memory.
- Never invent a figure, price, quote or customer: every figure is dated and sourced.
- Adapt to the site's platform (WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, code): ask if unclear, and deliver in a format it accepts.
- Keep the brand's voice.

## Pick the mode

- An existing article to improve, rank higher or update: **mode A**.
- Titles, metadata, click-through rate, truncated titles: **mode B**.
- What to write, what customers ask, visibility in AI assistants: **mode C**.
- A new article: mode C for the angle, then mode B for the title.

## Mode A: refresh an article

1. **Current state**: word count, date, main keyword, position and clicks (Search Console if available), H2/H3 structure, links, calls to action. Strengths, weaknesses, quick wins.
2. **Competitors**: search the keyword on Google. Note the top 10 titles, the featured snippet, "People also ask". Open the 5 most relevant pages: word count, outline, unique elements, date.
3. **Gaps**: table of the article vs competitors 1 to 5. Missing topics ranked: high priority (4+ competitors), medium (2 or 3), low (1), angles nobody covers.
4. **Plan**: figures to update, sections to add, FAQ, internal links, 3 title options, new outline with each H2 marked Kept, Modified or New.
5. **Approval**: present the plan and wait for approval before writing. Never promise ranking gains.
6. **Rewrite**: keep what works, the voice, URL and anchors. "Updated on [date]". Direct answer in the first two sentences. Short paragraphs, more H3s, tables where they help.

Deliverable: the rewritten article, then SEO title, meta description, internal links, images to produce, word count before and after, sections added.

## Mode B: titles and meta descriptions

The Google results page is the brief.

- Formula: main keyword + benefit or detail + brand.
- Query first, 40 to 55 characters ideally, 60 maximum. Meta description of 150 to 160 characters.
- Title, meta, H1 and URL aligned. One unique title per page. Never "Home" or a vague category alone. Do not change an existing URL for a year or a number.
- Patterns: guide, definition ("What is [term]?"), how-to ("How to [task] without [obstacle]"), list ("[Number] best [tools] for [audience] in [year]"), comparison ("[A] vs [B]: which to choose for [need]"), problem ("How to fix [problem]"). One pattern per page, no clickbait.
- Method: infer the keyword and intent, look at the titles that rank, write 3 to 5 options, keep the most accurate.

Deliverable: table Page | Current title | Proposed title | Proposed meta | Why.

## Mode C: customer journey and questions

Understand the offer and audience, then map the 5 awareness levels: unaware, problem-aware, solution-aware, product-aware, ready to buy.

For each level: the customer's mindset, 3 to 5 questions as they would type them into ChatGPT or Google (natural phrasing, taken from real research), and the content to produce. Ask a question only if the offer or audience is unclear.

## Final check

Every figure sourced, nothing invented, full deliverable.
